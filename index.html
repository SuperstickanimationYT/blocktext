Block Text

Edit a Scratch or PenguinMod project as text

Open an .sb3 or .pmp file, or drop one anywhere on this page. Each sprite's scripts turn into text you can edit, and saving turns the text back into blocks.

Scripts that use blocks without a text form yet stay exactly as they are, and show in the list under the editor. Your file never leaves this browser, and saving downloads a new copy.
